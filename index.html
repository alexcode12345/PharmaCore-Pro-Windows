Records are saved in browser storage and mirrored to a local IndexedDB database to survive reloads. Export backups regularly; clearing browser data, private browsing, or changing browsers/devices can still remove or separate records. This is not cloud sync. Do not use as the sole record system for a live pharmacy without appropriate security, backup, testing and compliance review.
